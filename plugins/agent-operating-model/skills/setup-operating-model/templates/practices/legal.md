# {{Project}} — Legal practices

Read by every lead and specialist who drafts, places or changes a legal or privacy text.

## Drafts, not advice

- Every legal text opens with a line saying it is a draft, to validate with a lawyer, and not legal advice. An agent's legal reading is never reported as "cleared".
- Each item states what changed, the team's draft reading, what the lawyer validates and which milestone it blocks.
- Laws are cited with their official URL; what was cited from memory goes in its own list.
- The owner's provisional choices go to the lawyer as questions, with their reasons.

## Published texts

- A published text is frozen: a change is a new version. Consent stores the version it was given to, and a consent box is never pre-ticked.
- The legal file is the single source. A test compares the page with it block by block; whoever builds the page writes none of its sentences. Where a prototype and the legal text differ, the legal text wins.
- Every claim in a privacy notice rests on an owner action or a measured behavior. If the behavior changes, the claim goes back to review.
