# {{Project}} — Product practices

Read by every lead and specialist who does customer research, finance, support or roadmap work.

## Customer research

- Ask what happened, never "would you use…". Show the product last. Praise is no signal; commitment is.
- The agent never contacts anyone. Each interview has a code; no names or contact data enter the repository.
- Record only on a clear yes; consent says what is kept, where and for how long.
- Every insight cites its source, and the synthesis is checked against the plan's validation threshold.

## Finance

- Numbers are run in code, never estimated in prose. Every input has a source and a date, every output shows its formula, and scenarios go in tables.

## Support

- Help content matches the product exactly, and promises nothing beyond the published terms.
- A request outside the product's scope is logged as a request, with no date promised. What isn't known is said plainly.

## Phases

- Each phase starts only when the previous one's numeric gate, set beforehand, is met; each gate also says what happens when it is missed.
- A deferred item gets a trigger of its own and is recorded as "owed, not done", never dropped silently.
