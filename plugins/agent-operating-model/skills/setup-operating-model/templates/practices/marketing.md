# {{Project}} — Marketing practices

Read by every lead and specialist who writes copy, works on search or AI visibility, or generates media.

## Promises

- Promise only what the product does today. Later features say "coming soon", with no date.
- A current limit is never stated as permanent: say it is not offered today, never that the product cannot do it. Never claim a scope (markets, launch order, billing timing) the owner did not state.
- A promise that carries conditions appears only in the FAQ and the Terms, never in a headline or next to a price.
- Each promise lives in one designated place, with one text.

## Numbers and claims

- Real numbers only, from the repository or the owner; never invented metrics. An estimate is labelled as one.
- A strong claim carries its qualifier in the same sentence. Nothing is called "anonymous" unless it is.
- Public drafts never carry secrets, customer data or internal URLs.

## Search and AI visibility

- One page per real use case; no thin mass-generated pages; only the canonical domain is indexed.
- Honest only: no fake reviews, no posing as a customer, no hidden text, no instructions aimed at AI models, no bot-only content.
- A list of tactics not to use is kept in this file (abuse of a free plan, popups, bought links, …).
- Demand is measured with free sources first, with their limit stated; buying a tool waits until free first-party data is not enough.
- No analytics that would make a privacy promise false.
- Before adding text to fix a misperception, find the site's own published text that causes it.
- (option: ai-visibility) A fixed set of customer questions, approved by the owner, is put to public AI assistants each month: verbatim, a fresh chat per question, no login, no accepting terms. The report shows the trend, not one reading; a change of wording restarts that question's series.

## Paid media generation

- A request for creative freedom becomes distinct creative directions, one prompt each, with only the hard constraints; the owner sees the directions first.
- Before any paid generation, the exact prompt, tool, model and estimated cost (per unit × count) are approved; then the agent stops until the yes. The balance is read before and after, and every generation is logged.
- No real person's likeness and no third-party marks.
