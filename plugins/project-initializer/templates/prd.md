# Template: `docs/PRD.md`

The product requirements: problem, target users, goals, non-goals, success metrics, constraints. High-level and stable; feature-level PRDs go in `docs/prds/<slug>.md`.

Format rules:

- Every section has real content from the survey or the user's answers, or an explicit TODO question. Never invent users, metrics or constraints.
- Success metrics are measurable.

````markdown
# Product Requirements

## Problem

<What is broken or missing today, and for whom.>

## Target users

- **Primary:** <who>

## Goals

- <Goal>

## Non-goals

- <What this project deliberately does not do.>

## Success metrics

- <A measurable outcome.>

## Constraints

- <Technical, legal or business limits.>
````
